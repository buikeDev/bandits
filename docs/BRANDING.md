# BAND-IT branding

- Write the public brand name as **BAND-IT**, including the hyphen.
- Use `apps/frontend/src/components/BrandLogo.tsx` for website logos. It recreates the reference wordmark with a yellow hyphen and the tagline **PEOPLE EVENTS BRANDS**.
- The logo inherits its lettering colour from its container. Use dark lettering on light backgrounds and white lettering on dark backgrounds.
- Use `tagline={false}` for small applications such as the customizer wristband mark. Keep the logo's proportions intact.
- The supplied mood board guides brand identity; it is not a replacement website layout.
- The homepage and product promo photographs include the corrected BAND-IT name. Keep the product preview plain and unprinted.
- Existing package scopes, database slugs, cookies and saved-cart keys retain their internal `bandit` identifiers for compatibility. These are not public brand labels.
- Catalogue displays normalize the legacy brand name, and the database seed uses BAND-IT for new or updated brand records.
