# BAND-IT

BAND-IT is an ecommerce and fulfilment platform.

## Primary Business

BAND-IT primarily sells event and identification wristbands:

- Paper Tyvek — plain and printed
- Vinyl/plastic — plain and printed
- Rubber/silicone — plain and printed
- Fabric — printed
- VIP wristbands
- Hospital/identification wristbands

## Additional Commerce

BAND-IT may sell products outside wristbands.

Some of these products can belong to businesses that use BAND-IT's
fulfilment service.

These products can receive visibility through the BAND-IT marketplace.

## Fulfilment

BAND-IT provides fulfilment services to businesses, including:

- inventory storage
- order processing
- picking and packing
- delivery/dispatch
- potential marketplace visibility

Fulfilment is a first-class part of the product and must NOT be treated
as a small secondary service hidden under a generic Services page.

## Platform Pillars

The platform has three major areas:

1. Wristbands
2. Marketplace
3. Fulfilment

Wristbands remain the flagship product.

## Brand

Minimal, modern and product-focused.

The website should primarily use neutral backgrounds and typography,
allowing colorful wristbands to provide most of the visual identity.

The design should feel professional enough for B2B customers while
remaining attractive to event organizers and individual buyers.
